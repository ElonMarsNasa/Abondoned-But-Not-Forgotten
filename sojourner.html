What is this rock made of?

Sojourner’s Alpha Proton X-ray Spectrometer investigated the elements in rocks and soil. Its sensor was at the rear of the rover.

Clues to an ancient flood

The mission investigated a floodplain. Rounded pebbles and other observations suggested running water in Mars’s past.

Today: dry ground preserves clues to a different past.
What do the clues mean?
A flood can carry and reshape rocks. Scientists compare rock shapes, chemistry, and the landscape to test explanations. The water scene is an illustration of the idea.
